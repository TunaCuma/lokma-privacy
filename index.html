Lokma privacy policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Lokma is a puzzle game for Android made by Cuma Labs. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use it.

In short

Lokma does not collect any personal data. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it sends nothing about you to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your phone

Your progress, settings and play history are saved only on your phone, inside the app. We cannot see them. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes them.

If Android backup is on, Android may include this data in your phone’s backup to your Google account. That backup is handled by Google under its own privacy policy, not by us.

Sharing

If you use a Share or Copy button in the app, the app hands your play statistics to the app or clipboard you choose. Nothing is sent to us.

Permissions and network

Lokma uses the vibration permission for short haptic feedback, which you can switch off in Settings. The current version makes no network connections.

Children

Lokma is not directed at children under 13. As it collects no data, it collects none from children either.

Changes

If a future version changes any of this, for example by showing ads, we will update this policy before that version is released, name the services involved and what they collect, and ask for your consent in the app where the law requires it. The date at the top shows the latest change.

Contact

Questions about this policy: tunacuma2003@gmail.com
